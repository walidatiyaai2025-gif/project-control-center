# Version History

Managed product projects may keep one canonical per-project version-history record here or at a profile-designated path. Entries map PRODUCT_VERSION → SOURCE_SHA/tag/build/artifact digest/Task IDs/user-review/production evidence and previous known-good identity.

This is evidence, not a place to invent historical versions. During migration, unknown history remains unknown; new immutable mappings are recorded forward from the verified baseline.
